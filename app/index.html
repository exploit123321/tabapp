    Settings

    Pen: draw · side button or eraser end: erase
    One finger: pan · two fingers: pan & zoom
    Two-finger tap: undo · three-finger tap: redo
    Frames: ◀ ▶ to move, ▶ on the last frame adds a new one
    Keys: ← → frames · 1–6 colours · N new frame · Delete delete frame · P / H / E pen, highlighter, eraser · V voice · Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y

    Voice commands (tap the mic, then just talk — English)

    “colour 1” … “colour 6”, or “black / red / blue / green / orange / white”
    “undo”, “undo 3 times”, “redo”
    “next”, “back”, “new frame”, “delete frame”, “duplicate”, “frame 5”, “first frame”, “last frame”
    “pen”, “highlighter”, “eraser”, “size 1” … “size 4”, “bigger”, “smaller”
    “onion on / off”, “show / hide previews”, “clear frame”, “export”, “stop listening”
    Chain them: “red and next frame”.
